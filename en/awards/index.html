Awards · Season I

Eight awards

Six categories, the Audience Prize and the Jury Grand Prix. The categories are not split by format: video, photography, GIFs, drawing and digital art compete on equal terms. Every award is an NFT on the TON blockchain.

02/ 06Categories

What each is for

Now one by one, from the centre outwards. The categories are not a ruler but a vocabulary: they name what is already in the work.

Principal awards

I

Author of the Year

The principal award of the season — for a body of work, not a single lucky post. The jury looks at how the posts add up to a line: quality and originality, a recognisable manner, growth from one work to the next, a contribution to the language of the genre.

  • Body of work
  • Manner
  • Growth

Decided by: juryEntry: required

II

Best Channel of the Year

The channel as a complete work: concept, visual system, the mix of formats — video, photos, GIFs, drawings — rhythm of posting, texts and captions. The community is judged not by its size but by the kind of conversation the author holds with the viewer.

  • Concept
  • Visual system
  • Dialogue with viewers

Decided by: juryEntry: required

03/ 06How we judge

Three levels, seven questions

The categories say what we reward. Next is how. The jury looks at the work, at the author’s position behind it and at the mark they leave. No level outweighs the others.

IIIContribution

What changed thanks to the author — in the language of the genre, the industry, culture, society.

IIPosition

What the author stands for: a view of the body, the partner and the viewer — and whether they keep to it in the frame.

IThe work

How it is made: seven questions of craft and language, below.

A flawless picture without a position is an advert. A bold position without craft is a slogan.

Rules of the awards, § 10

Level I · the work

Seven questions for every work

The full rulebook
  1. 01IdeaA concept of one’s own, not someone else’s trick done well. The work knows why it exists.
  2. 02ImageLight, colour, texture, space. What the surface of the work — a video, a photograph, a drawing — says before anything happens.
  3. 03StructureHow the work is built: the dramaturgy of a scene on video, the composition of a photograph or a sheet, the order of frames in a series. Where the tension goes and how it resolves.
  4. 04Point of viewPoint of view, literally: distance, angle, movement. Where the camera or the artist’s eye stands, the viewer stands.
  5. 05RhythmThe edit of a video, the loop of a GIF, the sequence of photographs, the pause between posts. Where the work holds, where it breaks off, and whether every transition is earned.
  6. 06SignatureRecurring decisions that add up to a manner. You know the author without a credit — on video, in a photograph, in a drawing.
  7. 07AfterimageThe after-effect: what keeps working once the work has been closed.

We don’t count

  • Looks
  • The jury’s own kinks
  • “Pretty or not”
  • Follower count
  • Views
  • Income
  • Advertising

This is not a beauty contest. Beyond craft, the jury judges the author’s position and their contribution to the genre, the industry, culture and society. Subscribers’ love has an award of its own — the Audience Prize.

04/ 06Two votes

Who decides

We don’t count followers, but we don’t take away their voice. So there are two votes, and they never mix.

Categories and Grand Prix

The jury decides

The jury watches channels in full — in sequence, not the best posts — and votes by category. That is what a year’s access to entrants’ private channels is for.

So do viewers

05/ 06The path of a work

From a link to an award

All together, five steps. None of them asks you to travel, show your face or send files.

  1. 01Author

    Entry

    Links to every channel, and up to three categories if you like. No files needed.

  2. 02Jury

    Viewing

    The jury views the whole body of work, where the author actually publishes.

  3. 03Jury

    Judgement

    Three levels — work, position, contribution — and seven questions for every work.

  4. 04Jury · viewers

    Voting

    The jury by category and for the Grand Prix; subscribers for the Audience Prize.

  5. 05TON

    Award

    Laureates are announced online; the NFT arrives in the winner’s wallet.

06/ 06The statuette

The golden hole

At the end of the path, this. A two-lobed ribbon circles the hole and turns one and a half times in a single loop, so it seems two ribbons twist around the opening. A form with no beginning and no end on a black marble plinth.

Why not metal

No metal needed. Memory is

The award is a small fragment of aura in the age of endless reposts. The collection of awards is an open chronicle of the genre that cannot be rewritten after the fact.

  • ITON

    It is visible

    The blockchain record is public: anyone can check who was awarded, when and for what. Recognition stops being hearsay.

  • IITON

    It cannot be forged

    Only the awards can mint it, and only once. A copy can be saved but never claimed.

  • IIITON

    It stays with you

    The NFT lives in your wallet and in Telegram. No customs desk can hold it and no search can seize it.
