02/ 07Authors · do I qualify

Five questions

Everything to know before you enter. The answers go nowhere: this is a hint, not a form.

Who can enter — in the rules
  1. 01

    Are you 18 or over?

    The awards are adults only: authors and everyone in the frame.

  2. 02

    Are you from a Post-Ost country?

    The former USSR, Poland, Czechia, Slovakia, Hungary, Romania, Bulgaria, Albania, the former Yugoslav countries.

  3. 03

    Do you create and publish the work yourself?

    Video, photos, GIFs, drawing, digital or 3D — any format and any mix.

  4. 04

    Is it gay content — men with men?

    The awards are for MSM creators, however they name themselves.

  5. 05

    Is everyone in the frame an adult who agreed to publication?

    The one border we do not negotiate. Consent can be withdrawn.

0 / 5

Answer five questions and you will know whether you can enter.

03/ 07How to enter

Four steps

Entry rules
  1. 01

    Check you qualify

    The five questions above: age, country, authorship, MSM and the consent of everyone in the frame.

  2. 02

    Gather your links

    Every channel and platform where you publish, private ones included. No need to send the work itself.

  3. 03

    Message us

    By message to the awards’ Telegram channel or by email. A template is on the right.

  4. 04

    Wait for a reply

    We will confirm we got your entry and tell you what comes next.

What to send

The key thing is links to all your channels: the jury will use them to see your work as a whole. Any format works — video, photography, GIFs, drawing, digital and 3D art — and any mix of them.

05/ 07Safe

The work is seen. You are not

The awards are built so that taking part costs nothing: no money, no face, no risk.

  • 01

    Under an alias

    We publish only a name or alias and the channel name. Anonymity here is not a whim but a working condition.

  • 02

    No documents

    No passport needed. We may ask you to confirm that everyone in the frame is an adult who consented.

  • 03

    Online only

    No ceremonies, no travel: entry, jury, vote and award all happen online.

  • 04

    Your work stays yours

    No files, nothing uploaded to the site. You can withdraw before the laureates are announced.

We are not looking for people to explain “what the author meant”. Sontag wrote that criticism should make the work, and our own experience, more rather than less real to us. A good review of porn does not hide arousal behind jargon. It says how that arousal was made.

06/ 07Jury · the panel is complete

The jury are critics

Cinema has film critics, painting and photography have art critics, restaurants have food critics. Porn barely has any. A review names a technique, compares, argues, and so builds the context in which a work becomes visible. The GOLDEN HOLE jury are the first authors of that context.

Who we chose

Taste over tenure

  1. I

    A trained eye

    Film, photography, exhibitions — not just porn. Taste begins with the ability to compare.

  2. II

    Attention to detail

    A juror notices the light, an awkward cut, a precise angle, the line of a drawing — and understands what they do to the viewer.

  3. III

    Language

    The ability to explain why a work works. Beyond “well, it’s hot”, but without academic armour either.

What a juror gets

  • 01/ 04

    A vote

    The jury decides the six categories and the Grand Prix.

  • 02/ 04

    Access

    A year of access to every entrant’s private channels.

  • 03/ 04

    Talking to the authors

    A direct conversation about how and why they film, photograph and draw.

  • 04/ 04

    Influence

    The jury’s reviews shape the language in which the genre will be discussed.

How they were chosen

An essay contest

  1. 01

    Viewers

    The candidates were subscribers of authors’ private channels, people with a trained eye.

  2. 02

    An essay

    Each wrote an essay on a topic announced in the official channel.

  3. 03

    A seat

    The authors of the best essays joined the permanent jury.

Jury selection closed · always looking for critics

Got taste? Write to us

Jury selection for this season is closed, but we are always looking for people with taste, opinions of their own and a trained eye. We are building a new institution — something like a Michelin Guide for a genre that never had one. It is made by those ready to discuss the work, write about it and argue.

Want to try your hand at criticism? Message the awards’ Telegram channel: tell us about yourself in a few words or send an essay.
