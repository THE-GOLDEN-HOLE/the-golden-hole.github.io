Season I · entries open

Two ways in

Show your own work, or judge someone else’s. Post-Ost authors send an entry; subscribers write an essay and join the jury. Both are free.

For authorsThe juryDates

For authors

Send your entry

Entry rules

  1. Check you qualify

    You are 18+, from a Post-Ost country, and create and publish your own content, in any format.

  2. Gather your links

    Every channel and platform where you publish. No need to send the work itself.

  3. Message us

    In the awards’ channel direct messages or by email.

  4. Wait for a reply

    We will confirm we got your entry and tell you what comes next.

What to send

The key thing is links to all your channels: the jury will use them to see your work as a whole. Any format works — video, photography, GIFs, drawing, digital and 3D art, NFTs — and any mix of them.

  • Name or alias
  • Country
  • Links to all your channels and platforms
  • Up to three categories
  • A few words about you and your formats

We are not looking for people to explain “what the author meant”. Sontag wrote that criticism should make the work, and our own experience, more rather than less real to us. A good review of porn does not hide arousal behind jargon. It says how that arousal was made.

Jury · now recruiting

Critics wanted

I want to join

Cinema has film critics, painting and photography have art critics, restaurants have food critics. Porn barely has any. A review names a technique, compares, argues, and so builds the context in which a work becomes visible. The GOLDEN HOLE jury are the first authors of that context.

Who we are looking for

Taste over tenure

  1. I

    A trained eye

    Film, photography, exhibitions — not just porn. Taste begins with the ability to compare.

  2. II

    Attention to detail

    You notice the light, an awkward cut, a precise angle, the line of a drawing — and understand what they do to the viewer.

  3. III

    Language

    You can explain why a work works. Beyond “well, it’s hot”, but without academic armour either.

What a juror gets

  • 01/ 04

    A vote

    You decide the six categories and the Grand Prix.

  • 02/ 04

    Access

    A year of access to every entrant’s private channels.

  • 03/ 04

    Talking to the authors

    A direct conversation about how and why they film, photograph and draw.

  • 04/ 04

    Influence

    Your reviews shape the language in which the genre will be discussed.

How to get in

An essay contest

  1. Subscribe

    The contest is held among subscribers of the awards’ private channels.

  2. Write an essay

    We will announce the topic and deadline in the official channel.

  3. Take a seat

    The authors of the best essays join the permanent jury.
