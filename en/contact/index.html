Fact sheet

Name
THE GOLDEN HOLE. In capitals, never translated.
What it is
An independent annual award for gay porn creators — gay adult creators — from the Post-Ost countries.
Tagline
Celebrating Gay Adult Art
Season
The first, 2026. Entries and the jury call are open; voting and award dates will be announced in the official channel.
Region
Post-Ost: the former USSR, Eastern Europe and the Balkans — from Prague to Kamchatka. The former GDR does not take part.
Formats
Gay adult content in any medium: video, photography, GIFs and loops, drawing and illustration, digital and 3D art, NFTs. Formats compete on equal terms.
Awards
Eight: Author of the Year, Best Channel of the Year, four role awards (active, versatile, passive, dominant), the Audience Prize and the Jury Grand Prix.
How it is judged
The jury reviews whole bodies of work against seven criteria: idea, image, structure, point of view, rhythm, signature and afterimage. Followers, views, income and looks do not count.
Jury
Selected through an open essay contest. The founders sit as honorary jurors.
Prize
A collectible NFT on the TON blockchain — a single edition carrying the date and the laureate’s name.
Entry
Free, aliases welcome. There are no sponsored categories. 18+ only: everyone on camera is an adult and consented to both filming and publication.

How to write about us

We call things by their names: gay porn is gay porn. But not “cinema”: the award covers more than one format.

  • WriteAvoidWhy
  • THE GOLDEN HOLEAvoid: “Golden Hole Awards”, “the Golden Hole prize”

    The name is never translated or shortened to “Golden Hole”: “the jury of THE GOLDEN HOLE”, “THE GOLDEN HOLE laureates”.

  • gay porn, gay adult contentAvoid: gay adult cinema, gay adult film, “the adult industry”

    The award is not only about video: photography, graphics, 3D and NFTs compete on equal terms. And we name the genre plainly, without euphemisms.

  • creator, author, gay adult creatorAvoid: actor, porn star, model, influencer

    We reward people who conceive, shoot, photograph, draw and answer for the work themselves. “Performer” applies only to the role awards.

  • award, independent annual awardAvoid: film award, festival, beauty contest

    It is an award with a jury and categories. Bodies are not scored — contribution is.

  • Post-Ost, the Post-Ost countriesAvoid: CIS, Russia, “Russian porn”

    Post-Ost is the former USSR, Eastern Europe and the Balkans, four alphabets. Not one country and not a political bloc.
